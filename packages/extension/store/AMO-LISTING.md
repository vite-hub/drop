## Description

```text
Drop puts a review toolbar on any website, even one you don't run.

Press Drop next to the address bar, then click anything on the page and write a note about it. Drop saves the note with a screenshot of what you saw, so you can find it later, share it, and hand it to the people who fix it.

<b>What you can do</b>
<ul>
<li>Comment: click an element and write a note. Each note keeps a screenshot, the page's address and the element you picked.</li>
<li>Highlight: draw on the page with a marker, a pen or arrows, and comment on it.</li>
<li>Measure: check sizes and gaps between elements.</li>
<li>See your notes on the page again when you come back.</li>
</ul>

<b>Where comments go</b>
If someone made a review of the site and shared it with you, your comments go to that review, and everyone on it sees them. Otherwise they go to your private notes in your Drop, one set of notes per site. You can switch in the comment list.

<b>Your Drop</b>
The extension works with drop.vitehub.dev, or with your team's own Drop: type its address on the welcome page.

<b>Privacy</b>
The extension asks for no website when you install it. It runs on a page only after you press it, or on sites you choose. It talks only to your Drop: no analytics, no ads, no tracking. A screenshot leaves your browser only when you press Send. In Firefox, press Drop again after the page changes, or turn on "On this site".

Firefox 128 or newer. Guide: https://drop.vitehub.dev/docs/extension
```
