# Privacy policy of the Drop browser extension

Published at https://drop.vitehub.dev/docs/extension#privacy. This file holds the same text: change both together.
[PRIVACY-CLAIMS.md](PRIVACY-CLAIMS.md) names the test that proves each claim.

**Who gets your data.** The extension sends data only to the Drop you connect it to: drop.vitehub.dev, unless you
choose another. Whoever runs that Drop holds what you send, under that Drop's terms. The extension sends nothing to its
makers or anyone else: it has no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

**What it sends, and when.** Nothing on a site until you press Drop there or turn on **On this site** or **On every
page**. With them on, it sends only what [What's sent, and when](https://drop.vitehub.dev/docs/extension#data) lists.
On a site where you have no notes and no review, Drop learns the site's address only when you press **Comment** or
**Highlight**.

**Screenshots.** A screenshot is taken only when you mark something for a comment, and leaves your browser only when
you press **Send**. It shows the visible part of the tab, without Drop's toolbar.

**What it keeps in your browser.** Your Drop's address, your sign-in, and your settings: where the hand shows, the
sites where Drop stays, and where comments go on each site. Your sign-in never reaches the websites you visit.

**Your notes.** Notes are private to you in your Drop's Browser notes folder. A review's comments are seen by the
people that review is shared with.

**Your control.** Every site permission is optional and can be taken back in the extension's settings or your
browser's. [Disconnect](https://drop.vitehub.dev/docs/extension#disconnect) the extension in its settings, or on
Drop's Agents page. Delete notes and comments in Drop.

**Questions.** Contact whoever runs your Drop through its contact page (https://drop.vitehub.dev/abuse for
drop.vitehub.dev), or open an issue at https://github.com/vite-hub/drop/issues.
